About

I have been a consultant at ACENSI since December 2022. On assignment at W-HA, a digital payments company, I work as Lead Engineer for Technical Integration and Payment Application Support. I support the integration of payment offers and analyse and resolve production incidents, in French and in English. Monitoring indicators and analysing data are also part of my daily work.

Before consulting, I was a web developer. After a year of self-study in 2018, I joined 3W Academy, where I earned the French professional title of web application developer and integrator in 2019 (RNCP, two years of higher education). During this training, I built PHP and MySQL websites following the MVC pattern; I then built a React website for Train & Perform.

From 2019 to 2022, I worked as a freelance web consultant: showcase and e-commerce websites, code reviews, project structuring, UX and UI. In parallel, from 2019 to 2021, I provided customer technical support at BeezUP, part of the Cdiscount group, in French, English and Dutch: product feeds and catalogues, API calls, SQL queries, PrestaShop, Magento and WooCommerce modules, technical documentation.

My first job was hygiene, quality and traceability control at AVS, from 2014 to 2016: inspections, partner certification, checks on product origin and compliance.

I speak French, Modern Standard Arabic and Dutch, and English at a professional level.
